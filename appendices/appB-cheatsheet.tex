\chapter{API Cheat Sheet}
\label{app:cheatsheet}

\noindent
Everything you would otherwise have to look up: imports, signatures and the
patterns that recur. Written to be photocopied, and to be the page you keep open
while working.

\chapterstub{
\textbf{Contents.} (B.1) Imports, grouped by what they are for --- the single
most common thing to get wrong after 1.0 moved things. (B.2) \code{asyncio}
patterns, one line each. (B.3) LangChain: models, messages, tools, structured
output, middleware. (B.4) LangGraph: state and reducers, nodes and edges,
\api{Command}, \api{Send}, checkpointers, interrupts, streaming.
(B.5) The .NET-to-Python concept table, repeated here so it is findable without
paging back to Chapter~\ref{ch:python}. (B.6) The measurement results tables ---
kept empty until each experiment in Chapters~\ref{ch:asyncio},
\ref{ch:tools}, \ref{ch:multi-agent}, \ref{ch:context} and \ref{ch:serving} has
actually been run. Plausible numbers are worse than no numbers.
}
